# Current policy verification

Read when official review dimensions, scores, venue eligibility, formatting, submission requirements, or desk-rejection risks matter. These links are discovery entry points, not a frozen policy snapshot.

## Official sources

- [ARR Review Form](https://aclrollingreview.org/reviewform)
- [ARR Reviewer Guidelines](https://aclrollingreview.org/reviewerguidelines)
- [ARR Call for Papers](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp)
- [ARR Author Guidelines](https://aclrollingreview.org/authors)
- [ARR submission checklist / common problems](https://aclrollingreview.org/authorchecklist)
- [Responsible NLP guidance and checklist](https://aclrollingreview.org/responsibleNLPresearch/)
- [ACL policy portal](https://www.aclweb.org/portal/), plus the target conference's official CFP, author instructions, template, and submission checklist.

With web access, check the current relevant documents before relying on them. Follow their current official links if these entry points move. Prefer aclrollingreview.org, aclweb.org, and official conference sites and documents they link to; do not substitute unofficial commentary for available official guidance.

Establish the target venue, year, ARR cycle, paper type/track, and submission stage from the request or artifacts. Review scientific issues while resolving missing context; ask only when a specific compliance conclusion depends on it. Distinguish ARR submission, conference commitment, and camera-ready requirements.

Record the applicable policy, source URL, access date, effective cycle if stated, and relevant clause for each consequential policy finding. If official documents disagree or have uncertain applicability, report the conflict instead of silently choosing a rule.

Label conclusions as **current official requirement**, **general review advice**, or **skill recommendation**. An official desk-rejection risk needs both an applicable verified rule and manuscript/submission evidence. If only the rule or only the submission evidence is available, describe the conditional risk and missing check.

Use numeric ratings only for dimensions whose current official scale, allowed values, and descriptors were verified; map the evidence to those descriptors and cite the form. Never cache score scales, page budgets, anonymity rules, checklist wording, deadlines, or desk-rejection conditions as permanent instructions.

If relevant official criteria cannot be verified, state `Relevant current policy was not verified`, identify what is unresolved, and use qualitative scientific assessments. Do not invent numeric scales or certify compliance. A copied older policy or search snippet alone does not establish current applicability.
